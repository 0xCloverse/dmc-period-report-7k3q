What happened

Results
  • Net income rose from $1,301M to $2,035M and the stock from $47.49 to $57.80. Both rank first of five.
  • Almost all of the rise is the Start R&D charge dropping out ($1,223M before tax). Every firm got the same kind of boost, so the ranking flatters the business.
  • Underneath, the business got slightly weaker. Costs rose while prices stayed flat, so gross margin fell from 25.5% to 24.7% of sales, and interest rose $94M.
Competitors
  • Firm B was the only firm to spend on R&D ($772M) and raised its Styling and Quality ceilings to 9. It already had the highest preference (26.6%).
  • Alec (Firm A) took the lead in microsegment 1E from Delite.
  • Boffo (Firm B) and Cafav (Firm C) ran short, and Beaut and Climax sold out. Demand is going unmet in the Family and Luxury classes.
  • Marketing spend is similar across firms ($377M to $436M). Firm A, not Firm D, carries the most debt ($10,539M).
Market
  • Car sales were sluggish in the first half of the year on economic worries.
  • Dealer truck sales are forecast to rise 13% next period (1,425k to 1,612k). Detonka is our only truck.

Our decisions and what they did

What we entered after Start and how it played out in Year 1, grouped like the Decisions menu in StratSim.

Overall

We changed nothing, and the scoreboard still looked great because the Start R&D charge dropped out. The business underneath slipped: flat prices against rising costs thinned margins, inventory ran down to 19 days, and $7.4B of cash sat idle while the loan cost 8.5%.
